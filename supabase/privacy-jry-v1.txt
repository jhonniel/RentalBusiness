JRY Rentals (“JRY Rentals,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, protect, and otherwise process personal information when you use the JRY Rentals website, rental platform, customer account, booking services, and related services.

JRY Rentals processes personal information in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission (“NPC”).

By using our website or rental services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

JRY Rentals is an equipment rental business providing rental services for equipment that may include Starlink Mini, DJI Mini 3, DJI Osmo 360, cameras, drones, connectivity equipment, and other rental equipment and accessories.

For privacy-related matters, JRY Rentals acts as the Personal Information Controller (PIC) for personal information that it determines the purposes and means of processing.

2. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Depending on how you interact with JRY Rentals, we may collect information such as:

A. Account Information
If you create an account, we may collect:
- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile number
- Account information
- Password-related authentication information
- Account preferences
- Privacy-policy version accepted
- Marketing preference

We do not store your password in plain text. Authentication credentials are handled by our identity provider.

B. Rental and Booking Information
When you rent equipment, we may collect:
- Booking ID
- Equipment rented
- Rental dates
- Pickup and return information
- Rental location
- Rental duration
- Rental status
- Equipment condition information
- Equipment serial number associated with the rental
- Rental history
- Payment status
- Deposit information
- Damage or incident records
- Signed rental agreement / waiver version and acceptance record

C. Contact Information
We may collect:
- Name
- Email
- Telephone or mobile number
- Delivery or pickup address, where necessary
- Communication history with JRY Rentals

D. Identity Verification Information
Where reasonably necessary to verify a renter or prevent fraud, JRY Rentals may request additional information necessary to confirm identity or eligibility to rent.

JRY Rentals will only request information that is reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

If identity documents are collected, they will be retained only as long as needed for verification, fraud prevention, or legal requirements, and stored with restricted access.

3. PAYMENT INFORMATION
When you make a payment, payment information may be processed through our payment methods or payment provider.

Depending on the payment method, this may include:
- Payment reference number
- Transaction ID
- Payment amount
- Payment date
- Payment status
- Payment method
- Limited transaction information

Where you pay by QR or bank transfer using details shown in the rental platform, JRY Rentals receives confirmation and reference information, not your complete card credentials.

JRY Rentals does not intend to store complete credit or debit card numbers unless there is a specific lawful and secure business requirement to do so.

Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AUTOMATICALLY
When you access our website, certain technical information may be automatically collected. This may include:
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device type
- Operating system
- Date and time of access
- Pages visited
- Referring website
- General usage information
- Login activity
- Security and authentication logs
- Waiver or agreement acceptance metadata, such as IP address and user agent, when you sign

This information may be used for website operation, security, troubleshooting, and fraud prevention.

5. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES
JRY Rentals uses cookies and similar technologies that are needed to operate the website.

If analytics or marketing technologies are enabled later, JRY Rentals will update this Policy and, where required by applicable law, provide appropriate controls for non-essential cookies.

6. WHY WE COLLECT YOUR INFORMATION
JRY Rentals may process personal information for the following purposes:

Rental Services
To create and manage bookings, process rental requests, confirm availability, manage equipment, arrange pickup or delivery, process returns, manage extensions, track equipment, and communicate with renters.

Payments
To process payments, confirm transactions, manage deposits, issue receipts, reconcile transactions, handle refunds, and detect suspicious transactions.

Customer Accounts
To create accounts, authenticate users, manage account information, display booking history, and provide customer support.

Equipment Protection
To record equipment condition, document damage, investigate missing equipment, manage lost or stolen equipment, and maintain rental records.

Communications
To send booking confirmations, rental reminders, notices about changes, responses to inquiries, and customer support messages.

Security
To detect unauthorized access, prevent fraud, protect the website, investigate security incidents, and maintain audit logs.

Legal and Regulatory Requirements
To comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests, establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, and maintain appropriate business records.

7. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
JRY Rentals will process personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis. Depending on the activity, this may include:

A. Contractual Necessity
Processing may be necessary to provide the rental service you requested. For example, we need your name, contact information, rental dates, and booking details to process your equipment rental.

B. Consent
Where consent is required, JRY Rentals will request your consent in an appropriate manner. Consent should be freely given, specific, informed, and appropriately recorded. Optional marketing communications are requested separately from the Privacy Policy acknowledgment and from the rental waiver.

C. Legal Obligation
We may process information when required by law.

D. Legitimate Interests
Where legally applicable, JRY Rentals may process information for legitimate business purposes such as security, fraud prevention, equipment protection, business administration, and service improvement. Such processing remains subject to applicable privacy principles and data-subject rights.

8. INFORMATION SHARING
JRY Rentals does not sell your personal information.

We may disclose or make personal information available to appropriate parties where necessary and lawful. These may include:

Payment Providers
For processing payments and transactions.

Hosting and Technology Providers
For website hosting, database services, cloud storage, authentication, email delivery, and security.

Delivery or Logistics Providers
Where delivery or collection of rental equipment is requested.

Service Providers
Companies that provide services necessary to operate JRY Rentals.

Legal and Government Authorities
Where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

JRY Rentals will take reasonable measures to ensure that third parties handling personal information on our behalf provide appropriate safeguards.

9. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
Some technology or service providers used by JRY Rentals may process or store information outside the Philippines.

Where personal information is transferred to another jurisdiction, JRY Rentals will take reasonable measures to ensure that appropriate privacy and security safeguards are maintained in accordance with applicable law.

10. DATA SECURITY
JRY Rentals takes reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, unauthorized disclosure, loss, destruction, alteration, misuse, and unauthorized processing.

Security measures may include access controls, authentication, role-based permissions, encryption where appropriate, secure database configurations, audit logs, backup procedures, security monitoring, restricted employee access, and secure software development practices.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

11. EMPLOYEE AND ADMINISTRATOR ACCESS
Access to customer information within JRY Rentals is limited according to business requirements.

Administrator: system administration access where necessary.
Staff and rental personnel: booking and equipment information needed to complete assigned responsibilities.
Customer: access to their own account and booking information.

12. DATA RETENTION
JRY Rentals will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing rental services, maintaining booking records, accounting, customer support, fraud prevention, equipment protection, resolving disputes, establishing or defending legal claims, and complying with legal obligations.

Signed rental agreements, waiver acceptances, and privacy-policy acknowledgments are retained with the related booking or account record for rental, accounting, dispute-resolution, and legal purposes.

When information is no longer required, JRY Rentals will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and business requirements.

13. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT
Under the Data Privacy Act, you may have rights including:

Right to Be Informed
You have the right to know whether your personal information is being processed and to receive information about that processing.

Right to Access
You may request access to personal information that JRY Rentals processes about you.

Right to Correct
You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

Right to Object
You may object to certain processing where applicable.

Right to Erasure or Blocking
You may request deletion, blocking, or removal of personal information where legally applicable.

Right to Data Portability
Where applicable, you may request your personal information in an appropriate electronic format.

Right to File a Complaint
You may lodge a complaint concerning the processing of your personal information with the National Privacy Commission.

Right to Damages
You may have a right to seek compensation where provided by applicable law.
